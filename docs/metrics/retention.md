# Retention Rate

## Definition
Retention rate measures the percentage of users who return to a product
after their first visit within a defined time window.

It is one of the most critical indicators of long-term product health —
acquisition brings users in, retention keeps them.

## How It Is Calculated

$$
\text{Retention Rate} = \frac{\text{Users who returned}}
{\text{Total users at start of period}} \times 100
$$

**Common retention windows:**

- Day 1, Day 7, Day 30
- Weekly cohorts
- Monthly active users (MAU)

## Who Uses This Metric

| Team | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Product | Identify drop-off points |
| Data | Build cohort analysis models |
| Leadership | Measure product-market fit |
